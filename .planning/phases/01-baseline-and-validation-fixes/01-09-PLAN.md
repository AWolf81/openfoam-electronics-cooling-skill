<objective>
Adiabatic back walls must not radiate (FIX-05).

Purpose: Found in 01-08: the default boundaryRadiationProperties entry `".*"` makes every boundary black (emissivity 1) at its boundary temperature. For open boundaries that is the room, which is correct. For the adiabatic back wall it is wrong: the full-height wall gave off 2.8–3.1 W net in the plate-fin case (9.22 W heat input), energy that does not exist, and the solids' energy check did not see it because it only balances the solids. The sink-only back wall (01-08) gives off 0.03 W, but the same rule applies.
Output: zero-net-radiation walls; an energy check that would have caught it; docs.
</objective>
