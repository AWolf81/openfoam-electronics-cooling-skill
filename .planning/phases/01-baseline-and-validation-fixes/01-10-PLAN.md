<objective>
Even polar ray count for fvDOM by default (FIX-06).

Purpose: Found in 01-09: with the default `radiation_rays: [3, 3]`, fvDOM's middle polar band straddles the horizontal plane, so faces facing straight up or down see 3/4 of the hemisphere; low-emissivity horizontal faces then absorb ~+80 W/m² that does not exist. Every balance still closes. On the plate-fin validation (coarse) R was 2.413 K/W (+5.8 %) with [3, 3] and 2.308 K/W (+1.2 %) with [3, 4]. The Infineon project runs used --fast-radiation [2, 2] (even) and are unaffected. User decision (2026-10-29): switch the default to an even count.
Output: new default, guard against odd values, plate-fin re-validated on both meshes, docs.
</objective>
