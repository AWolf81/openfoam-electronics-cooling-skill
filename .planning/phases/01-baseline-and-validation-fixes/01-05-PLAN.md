<objective>
Figure style aligned with the paper's figures, smoother (REP-01).

Purpose: The plate-fin paper's figures (Fluent rainbow, banded, legend left) read better than ours (inferno/IR palette, solid surfaces only). Decision (2026-10-28): use matplotlib's Turbo — the same blue→cyan→green→yellow→red look as the Fluent rainbow but with smooth lightness (no false edges) — with point-interpolated fields and fine levels, so curves are smoother than the paper's ~20 bands. Keep the IR palette only for comparisons with a thermal camera.
Output: one style used by all CFD figures; `report.style: cfd | ir` (cfd default).
</objective>
